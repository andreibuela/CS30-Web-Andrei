The Habituation Paradox

Traditional Cognitive Load Theory warns that background speech hijacks the phonological loop. However, empirical findings prove habituated students train their minds to strip semantic meaning from vocals:

  1. Selective Attenuation: Familiar vocals fade into harmless background rhythm.
  2. Lower Distraction: Lyrical listeners reported lower distraction (3.19/5) than instrumental purists (3.70/5).
  3. Emotional Regulation: Positive affect and dopamine outweigh the minor subconscious cost of auditory filtering.
"Silence makes me feel anxious or bored... music is not merely background sound, it is an essential survival strategy against burnout."
